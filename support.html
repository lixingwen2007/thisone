常见问题

我的转盘会同步到其他设备吗？

不会。所有数据只保存在当前设备上，App 不联网，也没有账号。

换手机后数据还在吗？

如果你用系统备份（iCloud 备份）恢复新手机，数据通常会一起恢复。

"不放回"模式是什么？

打开后，已经转到过的选项会被暂时排除，直到所有选项都被抽中一次，或者你手动重置。

结果真的是随机的吗？

是的。结果用系统提供的安全随机数生成，再按每个选项的权重计算，转盘动画只是把结果展示出来。

怎么关掉声音或震动？

在 App 的"设置"里可以分别关闭音效和震动。

FAQ

Do my wheels sync to my other devices?

No. All data stays on the current device. The app doesn't connect to the internet and has no accounts.

Will my data move to a new phone?

If you set up the new phone from a system backup (such as iCloud Backup), your data usually comes with it.

What is "No repeats" mode?

When it's on, options that have already come up are skipped until every option has been picked once, or until you reset.

Are the results really random?

Yes. Each result comes from the system's secure random number generator, weighted by each option's weight. The spin animation just shows the result.

How do I turn off sound or haptics?

You can turn sound effects and haptics off separately in the app's Settings.
