常见问题

点录音没反应，或者提示麦克风没有打开？

请打开手机“设置”，找到“倒放挑战”，把“麦克风”的开关打开，再回到 App 点“再试一次”。

播放的声音太小，或者从听筒里出来？

请检查手机侧面的静音开关和音量。App 会尽量把声音放大，并从扬声器播放；连着蓝牙耳机时会从耳机播放。

倒回来以后听不出原话？

这正是好玩的地方！多听几遍倒放、学得越像，倒回来就越像原话。短一点的句子更容易成功。

分享出去的是什么？

一段竖屏短视频：原声 → 倒放 → 你学的 → 倒回来，每段都有字幕。视频在你的手机上生成，由你自己选择发给谁。

我的录音会被保存或上传吗？

不会。录音只在手机的临时文件夹里，开始新一轮或重新打开 App 时就会删除，也从不上传。

FAQ

Nothing happens when I tap record, or it says the microphone is off?

Open the phone's Settings, find Reverse Challenge, turn on Microphone, then go back to the app and tap "Try again".

The sound is too quiet, or comes out of the earpiece?

Check the silent switch and the volume. The app makes quiet takes louder and plays through the loudspeaker, or through Bluetooth headphones if they're connected.

I can't hear the original after flipping back?

That's the fun part! Listen to the backwards sound a few more times — the closer your copy, the more it sounds like the original. Short lines work best.

What gets shared?

A short portrait video: original → backwards → your copy → flipped back, each with a caption. It's made on your phone, and you choose who gets it.

Are my recordings saved or uploaded?

No. They stay in the phone's temporary folder, are deleted when you start a new round or reopen the app, and are never uploaded.
