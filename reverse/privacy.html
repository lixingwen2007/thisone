隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 18 日

我们不收集任何信息。

  • App 需要使用麦克风，只是为了录下你说或唱的那一句，好把它倒过来放。只有你点了录音按钮才会录音。
  • 录音只保存在手机的临时文件夹里，从不上传。开始新一轮、重新开始，或者下次打开 App 时，之前的录音都会被删除。
  • 只有你自己点“分享这次挑战”时，App 才会在手机上生成一段视频，再交给系统的分享面板，由你决定发给谁。App 不会自己发送任何东西。
  • App 不联网，不包含广告，不包含任何第三方统计或追踪 SDK，也不需要注册账号。
  • 你可以随时在系统“设置”里关闭这个 App 的麦克风权限；卸载 App 会删除它的全部数据。

如果这份政策有变化，我们会更新本页面并修改上面的日期。如有疑问，请通过技术支持页面联系我们。

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

We don't collect any information.

  • The app uses the microphone only to record the line you say or sing, so it can play it backwards. It records only after you tap the record button.
  • Recordings are kept only in the phone's temporary folder and are never uploaded. They are deleted when you start a new round, start over, or next open the app.
  • Only when you tap "Share this challenge" does the app make a video on your phone and hand it to the system share sheet, where you choose who gets it. The app never sends anything by itself.
  • The app doesn't connect to the internet, shows no ads, contains no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and needs no account.
  • You can turn off the app's microphone access in the system Settings at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data.

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Questions? Reach us through the support page.
