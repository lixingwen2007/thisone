隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 15 日

我们不收集任何信息。

  • 你创建的转盘、选项、历史记录和设置只保存在你的设备上，不会上传到任何服务器。
  • App 不联网，不包含广告，不包含任何第三方统计或追踪 SDK。
  • App 不申请相机、麦克风、定位、通讯录等任何系统权限。
  • 你可以随时在 App 内删除转盘、清空历史记录；卸载 App 会删除全部数据。
  • 设备的系统备份（如 iCloud 备份、安卓自动备份）可能会包含这些数据，这由你的系统设置决定。

如果这份政策有变化，我们会更新本页面并修改上面的日期。如有疑问，请通过技术支持页面联系我们。

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

We don't collect any information.

  • The wheels, options, history, and settings you create stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server.
  • The app doesn't connect to the internet, shows no ads, and contains no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • The app doesn't ask for any system permissions, such as camera, microphone, location, or contacts.
  • You can delete wheels or clear history in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data.
  • Your device's system backups (such as iCloud Backup or Android Auto Backup) may include this data, depending on your system settings.

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Questions? Reach us through the support page.
